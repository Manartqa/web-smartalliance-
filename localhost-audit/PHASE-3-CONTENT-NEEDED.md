## 2. Axway page (`/services/axway`)

- **Partner status:** level or tier, and since when.
- **Products you implement:** API Management / Amplify, MFT (SecureTransport?), B2B Integrator.
- **Typical engagements:** licence resale, implementation, migration, support/MA.
- **Certified engineers:** how many, and which Axway certifications.
- **Reference project:** which projects can be cited (see section 1).

## 3. Service pages (to about 800 words each)

For each of the 7 services:
- **Who it is for:** government, enterprise, or which sectors.
- **What is included:** typical deliverables.
- **Timeline:** typical project length or phases.
- **Technology stack:** languages, platforms, cloud or on-prem.
- **Standards and compliance you work to:** for example the Electronic Transactions Act, PDPA, the Cybersecurity Act, ITA/EGA standards.
- **Case study:** one related project.
- **Common questions:** 3-5 that customers really ask.

## 4. "For government agencies" section

- **e-GP:** are you a registered vendor, and with which status or number?
- **Procurement methods:** which you take part in (e-bidding, selection, specific method).
- **TOR:** do you help prepare TOR or specifications before procurement?
- **Maintenance:** MA contract terms you usually offer, and your SLA response and resolution times.
- **Security and data handling** commitments for government data.

## 5. People and company identity

- **Leadership:** names, titles, and a photo if available, with permission.
- **Team:** size, and the main certifications held (vendor, PMP, CISSP…).
- **Registration:** company registration number (เลขทะเบียนนิติบุคคล) and tax ID, for the footer and contact page.
- **Online profiles:** LinkedIn company page URL and any other real profiles, for the schema `sameAs`.

## 6. Numbers that can be published

- Projects delivered (approximate is fine, e.g. "100+").
- Number of government agencies served.
- Number of staff or engineers.
- Systems in production or under MA today.
